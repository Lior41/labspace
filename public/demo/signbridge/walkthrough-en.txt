SIGNBRIDGE explores assisted communication in American Sign Language. This is a captioned walkthrough of real application screens. The current release is a research interface, not a working ASL translator.

Camera preview requires explicit activation. Short local videos can be played without upload. The walkthrough shown here uses a prewritten suggestion. No model recognized a sign.

The person can reject a suggestion and type a correction. Rejection does not invent a meaning or silently accept the prediction. Text remains in the current page.

The output contract and abstention policy are tested with synthetic data. That does not establish recognition accuracy. There are no invented confidence scores or performance claims.

A compatible licensed model, signer-disjoint evaluation and qualified ASL review are required. Signed replies need authorized recordings of complete phrases. No such reply library is bundled yet.

The project demonstrates accessible interaction, privacy controls and honest research boundaries. It is not a replacement for an interpreter. The repository documents exactly what is available and what remains blocked.
